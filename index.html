Why we're raising money

October is Pink October, the month we come together to raise awareness about breast cancer and stand beside everyone affected by it.

Hospice Malta is there for people living with cancer and other life-limiting illnesses, and for the families who love them. Their nurses, doctors and volunteers bring care, comfort and support into people's homes, so no one has to face the hardest days alone.

Today we're playing for something bigger than the trophy. Whatever you can give, big or small, goes straight to helping Hospice Malta keep doing this work.
